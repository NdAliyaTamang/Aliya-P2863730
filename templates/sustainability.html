Sustainability scenarios

Explore what could happen under assumptions about size-related returns and emissions per return.

This calculator does not measure OwnableFit's impact. Model accuracy is not converted into a return-reduction rate.

How the calculation works

Baseline size-related returns = orders × size-related return rate.

Potential returns avoided = baseline size-related returns × assumed relative reduction.

Potential emissions savings = potential returns avoided × emissions per return.

Percentages are divided by 100 during calculation. A 10% relative reduction in a 20% return rate produces an 18% return rate.

Interpretation and limits

Results depend entirely on the supplied assumptions. This calculation excludes implementation impacts, changes in customer behaviour and other effects unless explicitly represented in the assumptions.

Use low, medium and high assumptions to explore sensitivity. Do not present a scenario as evidence that the prototype has reduced returns or emissions.
